===================================================================
DEADLY NOODLES & FINAL MINUTES - CUSTOM VIDEO FILES DIRECTORY
===================================================================

You can place your video files directly in this folder:

1. Black Mamba Video:
   Save as: videos/black_mamba.mp4 (or .webm)
   Triggered whenever the user gets bitten by the Black Mamba.

2. King Cobra Video:
   Save as: videos/king_cobra.mp4 (or .webm)
   Triggered whenever the user gets bitten by the King Cobra.

3. Black Cobra Video:
   Save as: videos/black_cobra.mp4 (or .webm)
   Triggered whenever the user gets bitten by the Forest Cobra / Black Cobra.

4. Other Deadly Serpents:
   Save as: videos/<snake_id>.mp4 (e.g. videos/inland_taipan.mp4, videos/boomslang.mp4)

5. Afterlife / Death Video:
   Save as: videos/afterlife_death.mp4 (or .webm)
   Triggered whenever the countdown timer expires (00:00.00) upon entering the afterlife!

NOTE:
If no local video file is present, the app automatically runs its high-definition
built-in 60fps animated Canvas Video Synthesizer tailored to each snake's unique traits:
- Black Mamba displays its terrifying velvety pitch-black inky mouth interior and 12mph strike!
- King Cobra displays its towering 14-foot hood with royal chevron markings!
- The Afterlife Video displays a hilarious celestial ascension with a ghost soul and golden harp!

You can also click the "📁 Choose Video File" button directly inside the app to load
any video from your device in real-time.
