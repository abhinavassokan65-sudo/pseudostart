🚨 Official Snakebite First-Aid Protocol

  1. Stay completely still and calm. Physical exertion pumps venom through your lymphatic system exponentially faster.
  2. Call emergency services immediately (911 / 000 / 112 / 999).
  3. Immobilize the bitten limb with a broad pressure bandage (bandage from fingers/toes up toward the body, firm as for a sprained ankle). Splint the limb so joints cannot bend.
  4. Do NOT wash the bite site. Residual venom on the skin is used by emergency hospital staff to identify the snake species and administer the exact antivenom!
  5. Keep the bite site at or below heart level.
⚠️ WHAT NOT TO DO (CINEMATIC MYTHS):
• Do NOT cut the wound.
• Do NOT attempt to suck venom out with your mouth or suction devices.
• Do NOT apply an arterial tourniquet (causes amputation).
• Do NOT apply ice or electric shocks.
